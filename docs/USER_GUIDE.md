# 📖 User Guide | VozVerde TTS 🎙️

Welcome to **VozVerde TTS**, a neural text-to-speech application with a dark chalkboard aesthetic, handwritten typography, fluorescent green accents, male and female voices, and direct MP3 audio download.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

1. **Paste or Write Text**: Click **📋 Pegar texto** to paste from your clipboard, or type into the chalkboard area.
2. **Choose a Voice**: Select **👩 Voz Femenina** (Female) or **👨 Voz Masculina** (Male).
3. **Generate & Download**: Click **⚡ Generar Audio MP3 🎵** to hear the audio with the green oscilloscope visualizer, and download your `.mp3` file with **📥 Descargar Audio en Formato MP3**.

---

## 🎧 Audio Player Controls

- **Rewind (⏪ Atrasar 5s)**: Rewinds 5 seconds back.
- **Play / Pause (▶️ / ⏸️)**: Toggle playback.
- **Fast Forward (⏩ Adelantar 5s)**: Advances 5 seconds forward.
- **Restart (🔄 Reiniciar)**: Returns to 00:00.
- **Timeline**: Clean scrubber bar that adapts seamlessly to your screen width without overflow.
- **Volume & Speed**: Slider with dynamic mute icon and speed multipliers (`0.8x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`).
